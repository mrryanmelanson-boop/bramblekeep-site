Bramblekeep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12

Bramblekeep is a game that stores your progress on your device. We do not sell your personal information.

What stays on your device

Game progress (gold, buildings, troops, packs, settings) is saved in a local file. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check what you own and to restore purchases. RevenueCat receives an

anonymous app user id and your purchase receipts; we never receive your payment details or your name.

Ads

The game offers optional rewarded video ads (watch for a bonus). Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your

device's advertising identifier and ad interactions. On iOS the game asks for your permission before using the advertising

identifier (App Tracking Transparency), and shows Google's consent form where required (EU, UK). You can say no and still

play; owning Premium removes ads completely.

Random rewards and gear

Troop packs, chests and crafting give random rewards. The chance of every outcome is shown in the game before you buy or open anything (Shop, Odds). These odds and your progress are not shared with anyone.

Online play with a friend (Co-op and Versus)

You can play Co-op or Versus with a friend by sharing a room code. The first time you do, the game creates an anonymous guest account on our game server (hosted on Cloudflare). There is no sign-up, no email, no chat and no location.

The server receives: a random guest id; the display name and avatar troop the game picked for you (you cannot type your own name); the room code; the deck, troop levels and research levels you bring to the match; and the moves you make during the match, so both phones can play the same game. Your friend sees your display name, avatar and deck during the match. Match moves are only held while the match runs. Like any web service, Cloudflare processes network addresses to deliver the connection.

The guest account stays until you delete it: Settings > Delete my online data removes it from the server. Playing the practice match against the computer player uses no server at all.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple provides your alias for leaderboards. We do not store it.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Deleting your data

You do not sign up for anything. The only account is the anonymous guest account made for online play, which you can delete in the game (Settings > Delete my online data). See how to delete your data.
