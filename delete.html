Delete your Bramblekeep data

Last updated: 2026-11-11

Bramblekeep does not have player accounts: you do not sign up, so there is no account to close. Here is where your information lives and how to remove it.

On your device

Your progress (gold, gems, troops, research, gear, settings) is stored only on your device. To delete it, delete the game from your iPhone or iPad (touch and hold the icon, then Remove App, then Delete App). Nothing is kept on our servers.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check what you own, under an anonymous id that is not linked to your name or email. To have that anonymous id and its purchase record deleted, email us from the address below and include the app version; we will delete it within 30 days. Apple keeps its own purchase history, which is managed in your Apple ID settings.

Ads

Ad data is collected by Google AdMob. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.
