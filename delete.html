Delete your Bramblekeep data

Last updated: 2026-11-12

Bramblekeep has no sign-up. The only account is an anonymous guest account that the game makes the first time you play online with a friend. Here is where your information lives and how to remove it.

Online play (guest account)

In the game, open Home, then Settings (gear icon), then tap Delete my online data twice. This deletes your guest account and everything the game server holds for it (your random id, display name, avatar and any friend data). Your progress on the phone is not touched. If you have already deleted the app, email us from the address below with the app version and we will delete it within 30 days.

On your device

Your progress (gold, gems, troops, research, gear, settings) is stored only on your device. To delete it, delete the game from your iPhone or iPad (touch and hold the icon, then Remove App, then Delete App). The only thing our game server may hold is the guest account above.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check what you own, under an anonymous id that is not linked to your name or email. To have that anonymous id and its purchase record deleted, email us from the address below and include the app version; we will delete it within 30 days. Apple keeps its own purchase history, which is managed in your Apple ID settings.

Ads

Ad data is collected by Google AdMob. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in iOS Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.
